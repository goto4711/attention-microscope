    Experiments to try
    • With the starting preset, tap the verb, open WV and hide plural. The verb can still find its subject, but it no longer learns whether the subject is plural.
    • Set dhead to 1 and train Verb → its subject. One dimension has to encode both "I am a verb" and "you are an early noun", and accuracy drops.
    • Load the previous-token preset and hide both position rows in WK. Keys lose their position, and attention spreads out evenly.
    • Ask WV to carry six features with dhead 4. They can't all fit, so WVWVT stops being a clean diagonal: a small case of features competing for too few dimensions.

    Toy model: embeddings are hand-set features plus a two-dimensional rotary-style position (angle = position × 1 rad); WO is tied to WVT so output reads in the same feature terms. Sentences are generated from a 25-word vocabulary.
